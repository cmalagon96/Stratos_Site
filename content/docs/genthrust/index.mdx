# GenThrust

GenThrust is an enterprise automation platform purpose-built for aviation operations,
MRO (Maintenance, Repair & Overhaul) businesses, and companies running Microsoft 365.

## Modules

### Automation Hub

Automate repetitive workflows across Microsoft 365:

- **Power Automate** flows for document routing and approval chains
- **SharePoint** list-driven inventory management
- **Graph API** integrations for Exchange, Teams, and calendar sync
- **Scheduled reports** delivered to email or Teams channels

### MRO Operations

Tools specific to aviation parts brokerage and MRO coordination:

- Repair order tracking with status lifecycle management
- Vendor performance dashboards and lead-time analytics
- Parts inventory with reorder point alerts
- ERP integration connectors (SAP, NetSuite, QuickBooks)

### Client Portal

A white-label client-facing portal for:

- Real-time repair order status visibility
- Document upload and approval workflows
- Invoice access and payment via Stripe
- Secure messaging with support team

## Getting Started

### 1. Account Setup

Contact [sales@stratosstrat.com](mailto:sales@stratosstrat.com) for a discovery
call. GenThrust is deployed as a managed service — there is no self-serve sign-up.

### 2. Microsoft 365 Integration

Connect your Microsoft 365 tenant by granting the GenThrust Azure AD application
the following permissions:

- `Mail.Read` / `Mail.Send`
- `Sites.ReadWrite.All` (SharePoint)
- `Calendars.ReadWrite`
- `User.Read.All`

### 3. Custom Workflows

Workflows are configured via the GenThrust admin panel. Our team assists with
the initial workflow design and deployment during onboarding.

## Compliance

GenThrust data handling is aligned with NIST SP 800-171 controls and supports
customers subject to ITAR / EAR export regulations in the aviation sector.
